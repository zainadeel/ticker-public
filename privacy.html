Privacy Policy

Effective August 15, 2026

Ticker is a timer app developed and operated by an independent developer (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how Ticker handles information when you use the app.

Summary

Ticker does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share your personal information. The app does not contain advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. Timer data and app preferences are processed and stored locally on your device.

Information stored on your device

Ticker stores limited information locally so that the app can function:

  • A preference indicating whether the welcome screen has already been shown.
  • Timer state, including the selected duration, remaining time, timer status, rotation, and relevant timestamps.
  • Information needed to keep the main app, widgets, Live Activities, and App Intents synchronized.

Timer state is stored in an Apple App Group container that is available only to Ticker and its extensions. This information is not transmitted to us or stored on our servers.

Device motion

Ticker may use motion data from your device to create visual interface effects. Motion data is processed in real time on your device. Ticker does not store or transmit this motion data.

Notifications and Live Activities

If you grant permission, Ticker uses Apple system services to schedule timer notifications and display Live Activities. Notification and Live Activity information is generated from your timer state and processed on your device through Apple-provided frameworks. Ticker does not use notifications for advertising or tracking.

You can change notification permissions at any time in the Settings app on your device.

In-App Purchases

Ticker may offer optional, repeatable tips through Apple’s In-App Purchase system. These tips do not unlock app functionality and are not subscriptions.

Apple processes all purchases. We do not receive or store your payment card number, bank account information, or Apple Account credentials. Apple may provide transaction information, such as the purchased product, transaction status, and proceeds, as part of operating the App Store and processing the purchase. Apple’s handling of that information is governed by Apple’s own privacy policy and terms.

Third-party services

Ticker does not currently integrate third-party advertising, tracking, or analytics services. The app uses Apple-provided services and frameworks, including the App Store, In-App Purchase, notifications, widgets, Live Activities, App Intents, and device motion APIs. Information processed independently by Apple is governed by Apple’s privacy practices.

Data retention and deletion

Because Ticker does not operate a server or user-account system, we do not retain personal data about you on our systems.

Locally stored timer data is replaced or cleared as you use and reset the timer. You can remove Ticker’s locally stored app data by deleting the app from your device. Purchase records are maintained by Apple according to Apple’s own policies.

Children’s privacy

Ticker does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Ticker’s features or data practices change. Any updated policy will include a revised effective date. We will update our App Store privacy disclosures when required.

Contact

For privacy questions about Ticker, contact the developer using the support contact listed on Ticker’s App Store product page.
